# **App Name**: Sabor Station Menu

## Core Features:

- Dynamic Menu Display: Present 'Pasteles' and 'Tequeños' at $0.70 each, and 'Combos' at $2.50 or $2.70. Items will be grouped logically for clear navigation.
- Real-time BCV Euro Rate: Integrate and display the current BCV Euro exchange rate dynamically to provide pricing transparency.
- Highlight Special Offers: Visually emphasize combos and special offers to attract customer attention and facilitate easy discovery.
- Intuitive Menu Navigation: Provide user-friendly filters and categories to help customers quickly find their desired menu items.
- AI-Crafted Flavor Descriptions: An AI tool will generate engaging and evocative descriptions for menu items, aligning with the motto 'El punto exacto del sabor'.

## Style Guidelines:

- Color palette is anchored by the vibrant and energetic fast-food identity. The chosen scheme is dark to provide strong contrast. Primary Red: A bold and warm red (hue 5, saturation 90%, lightness 50%), converting to RGB hex '#FF160D', perfect for creating visual impact. Background: A subtle, dark charcoal-red (hue 5, saturation 20%, lightness 15%), converting to RGB hex '#322524', providing a sophisticated yet energetic canvas. Accent: A lively mustard yellow (hue 50, saturation 90%, lightness 60%), converting to RGB hex '#F2D20D', to highlight key information and call-to-actions, ensuring strong visibility against the primary and background.
- Headline font: 'Space Grotesk' (sans-serif) for a modern, bold, and energetic branding. Body font: 'Inter' (sans-serif) for exceptional legibility and a professional appearance in item descriptions and pricing.
- Utilize bold and simple food-related icons for menu categories and playful, expressive icons for special offers. Implement a euro currency icon next to the BCV rate for quick identification.
- The layout will be clean and modular, prioritizing clarity and logical grouping of menu items. Combos and special offers will occupy prominent sections or utilize larger, more visually striking cards. Ensure full responsiveness for seamless viewing across all device types.
- Implement subtle hover effects on menu items and smooth transitions for category filtering. Animated highlights will draw attention to promotional banners and combo details without being distracting.
